How it's worked out: borrowed per share = price × (1 − margin %). Shares you can buy = the most whole shares whose borrowed amount stays at or under your limit. Total amount = shares × price; you pay the margin part and the platform lends the rest.

Interest: where a platform has slabs, the slab is picked by the total borrowed and that rate applies to the whole amount. Rates are from each broker's published MTF pricing, checked Nov 2026, and change often, so confirm them in the app. Brokerage, GST and pledge charges are not included.

MTF margin % is the share of the trade you pay upfront. It differs by stock and can differ by broker, so copy it from your app. Your edits are kept in this browser.
